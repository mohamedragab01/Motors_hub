Short Description

The BMW X5 M xDrive is the high-performance flagship of BMW's renowned X lineup. It combines a potent twin-turbocharged V8 engine with BMW's intelligent xDrive all-wheel-drive system, delivering an exhilarating blend of raw power, precise handling, and premium luxury. Whether on city roads or rugged terrain, the X5 M commands attention with its aggressive stance, driver-focused cockpit, and cutting-edge technology.
