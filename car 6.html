Short Description

The all-new BMW M5 (G90) redefines the high-performance sedan segment by introducing an electrified M Hybrid system. Combining a monstrous V8 engine with an electric motor, the 2026 M5 delivers unprecedented power and torque. It is a masterpiece of engineering that offers pure racing DNA with the ability to drive in silent, all-electric mode for city commuting.
